GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got you.

A few things you may want to know before booking. Confirm all final terms with the rental team.

Ask us directly

Typically, a valid driving licence and government-approved photo ID are requested. Exact requirements depend on the rental provider.

Fuel policy can vary by vehicle and provider. Please confirm whether the car is supplied with a particular fuel level and how it must be returned.

Extensions may be possible subject to vehicle availability and approval. Contact the team before your scheduled return time.

Late-return charges and grace periods depend on the provider's rental policy. Confirm these details before your trip.

A refundable security deposit may apply. The amount, payment method and refund timeline must be confirmed with the rental company.

Outstation travel is subject to vehicle permissions, route restrictions and the provider's terms. Ask for approval before travelling.

Follow the provider's incident procedure and contact them immediately. Damage liability and insurance coverage are governed by the signed rental agreement.

Contact the rental team to request cancellation. Applicable deadlines, fees and refunds depend on the provider's cancellation policy.
